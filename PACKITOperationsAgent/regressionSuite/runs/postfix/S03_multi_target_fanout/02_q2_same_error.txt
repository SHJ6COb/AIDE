Are those two the same error, or different?

## Reply (verbatim, as rendered in the browser)
The two errors are related but describe different contexts:

The transfer to SAPP720110 specifies an invalid status for the T141 BOM item associated with material 6000.409.798 in plant 0780. This indicates that the material is not in a valid state for processing within that target system.

The transfer to SAPPOE0110 references a general update to the Cockpit data model and also points to the same issue with the T141 BOM item status being invalid, but without specifying the material status further.

While both errors originate from the same underlying issue of the BOM item's status, they pertain to their respective processes in different target systems.
